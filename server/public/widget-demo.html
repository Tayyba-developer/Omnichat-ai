OmniChat Widget Demo

This page loads the embeddable chat widget served by your backend. Click the green bubble (bottom-right) and say hi — the AI will answer from your product catalog.

How it works
1. widget.js renders the bubble on this page.
2. Messages POST to /api/widget/biz_margaret_7fz/message.
3. The backend saves the conversation and replies via Gemini.
